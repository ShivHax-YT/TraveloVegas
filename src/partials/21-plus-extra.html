Cannabis in Nevada: the rules

  • You must be 21 or older with a government photo ID. Out-of-state IDs and passports are accepted.
  • No public use.
  • Most hotels and casinos ban cannabis.
  • Planet 13 has an on-site consumption lounge, DAZED!, but you can only use what you buy in the lounge, not in the store.
  • Never take it out of Nevada or onto a plane.

From Planet 13's official FAQ, checked Oct 8, 2026. Rules change, so check before you go.
