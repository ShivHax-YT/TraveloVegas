Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It's cookieless and doesn't collect personal data, so it can't follow you around the web.

What your browser stores

We use your browser's local storage for two things only:

  • Remembering that you confirmed you're 21 or older, so the 21+ page doesn't ask every time.
  • Interface state, like your "Plan my night" picks.

This stays on your device and is never sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Services your browser talks to

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which handles basic request data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect it. Our fonts load from Google Fonts and our smooth-scrolling script loads from jsDelivr, so those services receive your IP address when your browser fetches them.

Affiliate partners

Booking buttons take you to VEGAS.com, Viator, GetYourGuide or OpenTable. Once you click out, those sites may set their own cookies, and their own privacy policies apply. See our affiliate disclosure for how those links work.

What we don't do

There are no accounts, sign-up forms or ad trackers on TraveloVegas, and we don't sell data.
