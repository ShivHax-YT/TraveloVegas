TraveloVegas is a free visitor guide to Las Vegas: shows, free things to do, food, bars and nightlife, pools, tours, family outings and getting around. It's run by a family in Las Vegas.

How we check listings

  • Every listing comes from a source we can point to, like the venue's official page or recent local news. If we can't find one, the listing doesn't appear.
  • Every card shows the date we last checked it.
  • When we can't confirm a place is operating right now, its card says "Not yet confirmed".
  • We only show prices and hours that a source states, worded the way the source words them.
  • When a place closes, we take it off the site.

Prices and schedules still change between checks, so always confirm with the venue before you go.

How the site makes money

Some booking links go to VEGAS.com, Viator and GetYourGuide. If you book through one of them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Many listings, including everything free, have no booking link at all, and our 21+ section never has booking links. Restaurant "Reserve a table" buttons go to OpenTable.

The details are in our affiliate disclosure, and what we store is in our privacy policy.
